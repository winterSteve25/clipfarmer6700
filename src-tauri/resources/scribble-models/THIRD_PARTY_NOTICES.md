# Bundled transcription models

- `ggml-large-v3-turbo-q5_0.bin` is a quantized Whisper model used by Scribble.
- `ggml-tiny-q5_1.bin` is the bundled lightweight quantized Whisper model.
- `ggml-silero-v6.2.0.bin` is the Silero voice-activity-detection model used by Scribble.

These files are application resources and are loaded locally at runtime.
